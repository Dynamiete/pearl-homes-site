Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about buying manufactured and mobile homes — pricing, payment plans, delivery, and customization.

How much does a manufactured home cost?

Our featured models range from around $50,000 for efficient single-section homes to $124,000+ for large multi-bedroom double-sections. Your tailored quote includes the home, options, delivery and setup — so there are no surprises.

Do you offer payment plans or financing guidance?

Yes. While we are not a lender, we walk you through common payment paths — chattel loans, land-and-home packages and cash purchase — and connect you with lending partners experienced in manufactured housing.

How does delivery and setup work?

We coordinate transport, placement, blocking and leveling, and utility tie-in guidance. Delivery timelines and costs depend on your site and the model — your quote will spell it all out before you commit.

Can I customize finishes, layouts or options?

Many models offer choices in cabinetry, countertops, appliances, exterior colors, fireplaces, dens and glamour baths. Tell us your must-haves in the quote form and we’ll match you to the right floor plan and options.

What brands do you carry?

We source from established, trusted manufacturers including Clayton, TRU, Giles and Kabco — covering single-section homes from 400+ sq ft up to large multi-section family homes over 2,200 sq ft.

Do you work with investors and businesses?

Absolutely. We support investors, landowners, communities and businesses with multi-unit pricing, phased delivery and consistent specs across units. Bulk purchasing power is one of our biggest advantages.

How do I get a quote?

Head to our Contact page, tell us which model you like (or describe your needs), and we’ll send a tailored product quote — usually within one business day.

Where do you deliver?

From our base in Pearl, Mississippi, we coordinate delivery across the region. Share your site address in your quote request and we’ll confirm logistics, access requirements and timing for your location.

Still have questions?

Send us a message and we’ll get back to you quickly.
